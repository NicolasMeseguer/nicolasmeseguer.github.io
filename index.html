About

I am a computer architect. In practice, that means I spend most of my day waiting for simulations to finish.

Ph.D. student in Computer Architecture within the CAPS group at the University of Murcia, advised by Manuel E. Acacio and José L. Abellán, funded by Fundación Séneca. I study how GPUs move data asynchronously through the Tensor Memory Accelerator (TMA) and the ecosystem built around it: Tensor Cores, Tensor Memory (TMEM), and so on. My research proposes ways to improve performance by efficiently overlapping the load-compute-store pipeline and achieving higher throughput. Most of that work happens inside MGPUSim.

I completed my M.S. in High Performance Architectures and Supercomputing at the University of Murcia in 2022, and my B.S. in Computer Engineering in 2021, where I received the Extraordinary Award for the best academic performance.
